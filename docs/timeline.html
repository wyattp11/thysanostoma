SST is a 4-point Hawaiian-waters monthly mean (Kona, Maui, Oʻahu, Kauaʻi offshore cells); heavier line = 12-month rolling mean. Background bands: El Niño (orange) / La Niña (blue) years by annual-mean ONI. Cyclones = made landfall or passed within ~200 nmi of the main islands (curated CPHC list). Sighting counts are effort-driven — the increase over time mainly reflects observers, not abundance. Sightings are read live from the site's dataset.
